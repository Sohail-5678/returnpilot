# International Returns Policy

## 8. International orders

### 8.1 Return shipping
International orders (shipped outside the United States) can be returned within the same windows as US orders, but return shipping is paid by the customer.

### 8.2 Original shipping
Original shipping costs on international orders are not refunded, unless the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item (see §5.2).

### 8.3 Duties and taxes
Import duties and taxes charged by your country are not refundable by Northwind Outfitters. International refunds can take a few extra days to appear.
