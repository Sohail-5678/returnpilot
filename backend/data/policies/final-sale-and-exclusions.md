# Final Sale and Exclusions Policy

## 6. Final sale and exclusions

### 6.1 Final-sale items
Items marked "final sale" on the product page and order can't be returned or refunded.

### 6.2 Damaged final-sale items
If a final-sale item arrives damaged, we exchange it for the same item. Final-sale items are never refunded.

### 6.3 Gift cards
Gift cards can't be returned or refunded.

### 6.4 Exceptions
If you believe your situation deserves an exception (for example, a return a few days past the window), ask for one. A team member reviews every exception request; ReturnPilot can't approve exceptions on its own.
