# Damaged or Wrong Items Policy

## 5. Damaged or wrong items

### 5.1 Reporting window
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, tell us within 60 days of delivery. This applies to every loyalty tier.

### 5.2 What we refund
For damaged or wrong items we refund the item price and your original shipping cost for that item.

### 5.3 Low-value items
If a damaged or wrong item cost less than $20, you don't need to send it back. We refund it without a return.

### 5.4 Photos
A team member may ask for a photo of the damage to improve our packaging. Photos are optional and never required for a refund.
