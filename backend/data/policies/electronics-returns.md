# Electronics Returns Policy

## 7. Electronics

### 7.1 Unopened electronics
Unopened electronics in their sealed original packaging can be returned within your normal return window.

### 7.2 Opened electronics
Opened electronics can be returned only if they are damaged or defective. Opened electronics that work as intended can't be returned.

### 7.3 Activated devices
Some devices report when they are activated. If our records show a device was activated, a team member confirms the return before it is approved.
